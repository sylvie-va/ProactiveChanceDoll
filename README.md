Adjusts Chance Dolls in Risk of Rain 2 to allow more proactive play in multiplayer. 

No more foregoing Shrines of Chance because one person in your lobby has a Chance Doll. Nor will the player holding the Chance dolls be forced into buying out Shrines of Chance first.

**Building the project:**

1. Check `~/ProactiveChanceDollProject/ProactiveChanceDoll.csproj` for build dependencies under game-libs & libs.

2. run `dotnet build ProactiveChanceDoll.slnx`
